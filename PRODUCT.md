# PRODUCT.md

General description of the product. It is intentionally non-specific: the owner will define details gradually. Do not treat anything missing here as permission to invent it.

## What it is

A small, private application that helps a business keep the prices of its products consistent with a changing exchange rate. The user records the products, and the app keeps track of how the rate affects what each product earns or loses.

## Who it is for

A closed group of two or three people who run the same business. It is not a public product and it is not open to the general public.

## The problem it addresses

When the exchange rate moves, the real margin on each product moves with it, often without anyone noticing in time. The users need to know quickly, and on the devices they already carry, so they can decide whether to raise or lower their own prices.

## What it does, in general terms

- Lets the users register products by hand or load them from spreadsheet and text documents.
- Follows the exchange-rate reference published by El Toque and keeps the products' derived values up to date as the rate changes.
- Shows, for each product, how it stands against the current rate.
- Alerts the users on their phones and laptops when the rate moves.
- Leaves every pricing decision to the people using it.

## What it is not

- It does not set or change selling prices on its own.
- It does not present the rates as its own data. The source is always credited.
- It is not a marketing site, a store or a public service.

## Where it is used

Android phones and Windows laptops.

## Look and feel

Clear and easy to see. Light mode. The main colors are chosen so that a person with normal vision sees them easily, and nothing more than that. The interface should not look generated by an AI (see `AGENTS.md`, Part C).

Tone of voice and anti-references beyond that: not defined yet.

## Status

Under definition. Details such as product fields, calculation rules, import formats, notification rules and interface language are decided step by step with the owner. The list of open decisions lives in `AGENTS.md`, section B.11.
